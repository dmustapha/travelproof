# Gap Map — BLI Legal Tech Hackathon 2

Primary source: `research/roster-audit/strategy-density.md` (real 16-build per-cluster map) + `research/research-brief.md` competitor/ecosystem sections. Corpus used GENERATIVELY (aim into whitespace), not just as a collision filter.

## Occupied (a competitor/cluster already owns — avoid or must clearly beat)
- **AI trading-agent + auditable verdict** (~5: Bull v. Bear, Debate Desk, Verdict Desk, Warrant, SignalLedger) — crypto-judge-facing, not lawyer-facing. SATURATED, near-duplicate.
- **Agent spending-limits / bounded authority** (~3: Foreman, PocketChange, Warrant) — Arc-native, crypto-ops-facing.
- **x402-paid CRE workflow** (~3: x402-Paid CRE, Legal Notary, ArcBooks) — x402 is the differentiator, not legal depth.
- **Compliance / sanctions screening** (~3: ClearRelease [strongest real-legal competitor], ZK-CID, Legal Notary KYB) — ClearRelease screens at signing/payment continuously, fails closed, on-chain evidence. Does NOT cite specific regulatory frameworks.
- **Evidence / notarization** (~4: PREDGE dispute-evidence, LexProof contract-review→Legal Passport, ArcShield, BlockDNS) — LexProof + PREDGE are genuine legal competitors; neither cites statutory frameworks.

## Whitespace (unoccupied positions the judging criteria reward)
1. **RWA tokenization with a cited legal wrapper (SPV, UCC Art.12 control, title-vs-token gap)** — 0 builds. RWA track is EMPTY.
2. **Regulatory-reporting automation (SAR narrative/FinCEN, FATF Travel Rule filing, MiCA CASP obligation)** — 0 builds. The workflow compliance officers actually run.
3. **Legal trust/escrow three-way reconciliation (IOLTA-style, engagement-letter-enforced disbursement)** — 0 builds. Mirrors HakiChain (BLI H1 winner shape).
4. **CRE Confidential HTTP (enclave) for privacy-preserving KYB / GDPR data-minimisation** — 0 strong builds. The sponsor capability the field ignores.
5. **Engagement-letter / contract-lifecycle enforcement (milestone gates, breach detection, automated notice)** — 0 builds.

## Missing outcomes (jobs-to-be-done nobody serves)
- A **human-readable, lawyer-FILABLE output** as the first-class deliverable (every build hashes AI output on-chain; none produce the document a lawyer signs and files).
- **Cited statutory frameworks** as the credibility layer (FATF Rec.16, MiCA, UCC Art.12, FinCEN SAR, GLEIF/vLEI) — absent across all 16.

## Portability gaps
- CRE Confidential HTTP works for in-enclave PII handling (privacy-preserving legal data) — proven capability, not yet ported to any legal-PII workflow here.

**The wedge:** ONE complete legal workflow whose PRIMARY artifact is a cited, lawyer-filable document, with on-chain proof as the trust layer not the product. That single discipline is what the 16-build field is missing and what BLI's judges demonstrably reward (HakiChain).
